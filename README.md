# Emerald-Connect-EHS-

Emerald Connect — a centralized platform designed to improve how students and schools manage clubs and organizations.

Emerald Connect allows students to explore clubs, discover opportunities, and join organizations, while making the club chartering and management process more efficient for schools. Instead of relying on manual processes and scattered information, Emerald Connect brings everything into one streamlined platform.

The goal is simple: make student involvement easier while helping schools manage their clubs more effectively.
